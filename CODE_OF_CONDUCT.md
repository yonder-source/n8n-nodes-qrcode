# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make this project welcoming, safe, and equitable for everyone.
Participation in good faith is open to all, regardless of race, ethnicity,
caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex
or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language,
philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socioeconomic position,
education, or other status.

## Encouraged behaviors

Participants are expected to:

1. Respect the purpose of the project and its ways of gathering.
2. Engage kindly and honestly with others.
3. Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
4. Take responsibility for their actions and contributions.
5. Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
6. Commit to repairing harm when it occurs.

## Restricted behaviors

The following behaviors, including threats or promotion of them, are not
allowed:

- Harassment or unnecessary personal attention after a clear request to stop.
- Insults, demeaning comments, character attacks, or discrimination.
- Inappropriately intimate or sexualized behavior in the project context.
- Sharing or acting on private information without permission.
- Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm.
- Impersonating others or evading enforcement actions through a false identity.
- Failing to credit sources for contributed content.
- Promotional or commercial material outside normal project communication.
- Irresponsibly presenting content that includes or promotes restricted
  behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to the repository and its issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews, and project-managed communication channels. It also
applies when someone officially represents the project in public or other
spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a possible violation privately to the repository maintainer through
GitHub, including relevant context and links. Reports will be investigated and
handled as confidentially as practical.

The repository maintainer may use the following enforcement steps, depending
on severity and impact: private warning, temporary limitation of activities,
temporary suspension, or permanent ban. These steps are guidelines; the
maintainer may use judgment to protect the project community. Enforcement
actions are handled privately with the involved parties whenever practical.
